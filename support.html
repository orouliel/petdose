Please do not send emergency pet health questions here. Contact your veterinarian or an emergency clinic. PetDose is a reminder and logging tool and gives no medical or veterinary advice.

Getting started

  • Add a pet, add a medication with its amount and unit, then set one or more schedule times.
  • The Today screen groups doses into Due now, Upcoming and Completed today.
  • Tap Given or Skipped to log a dose. Overdue doses stay on Today until you log them.
  • No account is needed to start. Tap Not now and everything stays on your device.

Account and sign-in

  • Guest mode works without an account and keeps 1 pet and 2 active medications on the device.
  • Sign in with email or with Apple to sync across devices, share a household, or restore Pro.
  • Trouble signing in? Make sure you are using the same method you registered with. If you used Sign in with Apple, use that button rather than email.

Reminders are not arriving

  • Check iOS Settings, then PetDose, then Notifications, and confirm Allow Notifications is on.
  • Check that a Focus or Do Not Disturb mode is not silencing them.
  • Confirm the schedule time on the medication is what you expect. Times use your device's local time.
  • Reminders are local to each device, so a dose scheduled on one phone notifies that phone.

PetDose Pro

Pro unlocks household sharing with a co-carer, dose report CSV export, full dose history, and unlimited pets and medications.

If Pro did not unlock after reinstalling or changing device, open Account and tap Restore purchases. Restore is also on the upgrade screen.

Household sharing

A Pro subscriber can invite a co-carer by email so you both log doses for the same pets. The person you invite must accept with the same email address the invite was sent to. A co-carer does not need their own Pro subscription to accept.

Deleting your account or data

  • Signed in: open Account from the person icon in the Today header, scroll to Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. This removes your pets, medications, photos, dose history and the account itself.
  • Guest mode: delete pets in the app, or uninstall PetDose to remove local data.
  • You can also email us to request deletion or a copy of your data.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel that in your Apple subscription settings.

Important

PetDose is for pets only: dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, reptiles, fish and others. It is not for human medication and does not replace your veterinarian. Always follow your vet's written instructions for amounts and timing.
