Privacy Policy

Effective 19 September 2026

This policy explains how Or Ouliel, operating as an individual Apple Developer, collects, uses and shares information in the PetDose mobile app.

PetDose is a pet medication reminder. It is not a veterinary medical device, it does not provide medical or veterinary advice, and it is not intended for human medications.

2. What we collect

2.1 Guest mode, no account

You can use PetDose without an account. In guest mode your data stays on your device:

  • Pet profiles, such as name, species and notes, up to 1 pet
  • Medications and schedules, up to 2 active medications
  • Dose history recorded on the device

Guest data never leaves your phone unless you later sign in and choose to sync it.

2.2 When you have an account

Sign-in options are email and Sign in with Apple. With an account we process:

  • Account identifiers: your email address, your Apple user identifier, and Apple's private relay address if you use Hide My Email
  • Authentication and session data needed to keep you signed in
  • Pet profiles, pet photos, medications, schedules and dose history synced to our database
  • Support messages you send us

2.3 Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. We and our billing provider RevenueCat receive subscription status and the identifiers needed to unlock Pro. We never receive your card number.

2.4 Reminders

PetDose uses local notifications only. Dose reminders are scheduled by your device and delivered by iOS. We do not operate a push server and we do not collect push tokens.

2.5 Technical data

Our providers may process limited technical data such as app version and device type in the course of serving requests. PetDose contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting SDK, and we do not build advertising profiles.

2.6 Household sharing

Pro users can invite a co-carer by email. If you use this, we process the invited email address and household membership so the people you invite can see and log doses for the pets you share.

3. How we use data

  • Provide reminders, schedules and dose logging
  • Sync your data when you are signed in
  • Authenticate you and secure your account
  • Unlock and manage Pro subscriptions
  • Respond to support and privacy requests
  • Keep the app secure and reliable, and meet legal obligations

We do not sell personal data, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

4. Legal bases, UK GDPR and GDPR

  • Legitimate interests: security, reliability and preventing abuse
  • Consent: where required, such as notification permission on your device
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or disclose information

6. Retention

  • Guest data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app
  • Account data is kept while your account is active
  • After deletion we remove your data promptly, except limited records we must keep for legal or accounting reasons
  • Subscription records may be retained for reconciliation with Apple and RevenueCat

7. Security

We rely on reasonable technical and organisational measures, including provider features such as encryption in transit, row-level security and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children

PetDose is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided data, email petdose.support@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. Your rights and deleting your account

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, and to object to certain processing. You can also complain to your local data protection authority, such as the UK ICO.

Delete your account from inside the app. Open Account from the person icon in the Today header, scroll to Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. This removes your pets, medications, photos, dose history and the account itself.

In guest mode, delete your pets in the app or uninstall it to remove local data. You can also email petdose.support@gmail.com to request deletion or a copy of your data.

Subscriptions are managed by Apple and are not cancelled by deleting your account. Cancel in Settings, your name, then Subscriptions.

10. International transfers

Our providers, including Supabase, RevenueCat, Apple and Expo, may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required we rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

11. Veterinary disclaimer

PetDose provides reminders and logging only. It does not diagnose, treat or prescribe, and it is not a substitute for a licensed veterinarian. Always follow your vet's instructions for medications, dosages and care. Do not use PetDose for human medications.
